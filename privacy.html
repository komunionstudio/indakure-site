Privacy

What we collect

  • Your email address or mobile number, only if you type it into a “Get notified” or “Subscribe” form.
  • Your age confirmation. If you tick “Remember me for 30 days”, your browser stores a small note so you are not asked again. It stays on your device.
  • Basic technical data that every website host records, such as the pages requested and the time of the request.

How we use it

We use your email or number only to send you news about IndaKure: the build, our opening date, events and classes. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it except with the services we use to store it and send our updates.

Text messages

If you give us a mobile number, you agree to receive occasional text updates from IndaKure. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help.

Unsubscribing and deleting your information

Use the unsubscribe link in any email, reply STOP to any text, or email us and we will remove you from our list.

Age

This site is for adults 21 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

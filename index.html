About

Your friendly neighborhood flower dispensary

IndaKure is an adult-use cannabis dispensary and garden, with genuine botanical knowledge, warm hospitality and a knowing sense of humor. Built by two sisters who really love plants.

Tended with care. Open to all: the curious first-timer, the veteran, the neighbor, the regular.

Hand-picked

Thoughtfully chosen adult-use flower from growers we trust, and honest guidance about where it came from.

Let's talk flower

Clear answers on flower, formats and terms. We teach before we sell.

Our story

Two green thumbs

We've always been plant people. The kind who get excited about new leaves, spend a little too long in the garden, and believe there's something special about watching things grow.

Inda comes from indica. Kure is our family name. Good flower, good people, and a whole lot of heart.
